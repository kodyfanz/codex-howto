## Screenshot policy

No product screenshots are distributed in the current guide. Codex surfaces
change frequently, and the lessons use commands, configuration, original
diagrams, and explicit verification steps instead. Temporary browser captures
used for local visual quality assurance are not release assets.

If a screenshot is added later, record its creator, capture date, product
surface, redactions, and license here. Never present an unverified or
third-party screenshot as a Codex product screenshot.

## Release exclusions

The release archive excludes `.DS_Store`, coverage output, caches, and
generated working directories.
