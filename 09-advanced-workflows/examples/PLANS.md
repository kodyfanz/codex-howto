# Execution plans

Use this template for multi-turn implementation work. Keep it understandable to
a contributor who has only the repository and this file.

## Goal

State the observable outcome and who benefits.

## Scope and non-goals

- In scope:
- Out of scope:

## Current evidence

List the files, commands, product behavior, and constraints already verified.
Distinguish facts from assumptions.

## Proposed approach

Describe the design, important interfaces, and why this approach fits the
constraints. Record alternatives only when they materially affect the decision.

## Progress

- [ ] First independently verifiable milestone.
- [ ] Second independently verifiable milestone.
- [ ] Integration and final review.

Update this list as work changes. Do not mark a milestone complete without its
verification evidence.

## Verification

For each milestone, name the exact test, command, or manual observation and the
expected result.

## Risks and recovery

Identify data loss, compatibility, security, rollout, and external-state risks.
Explain how to stop, revert, or recover safely.

## Decisions and discoveries

Record dated decisions or unexpected findings that change the plan.

## Handoff

Summarize completed work, remaining work, current checkout or environment,
uncommitted changes, active processes, and any approval still required.
